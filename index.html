About the game

Voltsphere is a puzzle game where you slide an energy sphere across a neon grid. It never stops until something blocks it, and it lights up every tile it crosses. The goal is to light the whole grid in as few moves as you can.

  • 1000 levels across 20 sectors
  • Plays offline, no account needed
  • English and Turkish
